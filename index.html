Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

In short

QR Studio has no accounts and no servers of its own. The codes you scan and create, your history and your settings stay on your device. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads at the moment.

1. Who we are

QR Studio (“the app”) is a QR code scanner and generator for Android, published by its independent developer (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at usmaniftikhar2803@gmail.com.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.
  • We do not upload the content of the codes you scan or create to any server of ours.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking, and the app currently shows no ads.

3. What is stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • History: the text of codes you scanned or chose to save, their type, date and your favorites.
  • Settings: your chosen language.

You can delete your history at any time in Settings → Delete all history. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app also removes this data.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera: to scan QR codes and barcodes. The camera image is processed on your device.
  • Photos / media: only when you choose an image to scan or to use as a logo (through the system picker), or when you save a generated QR code to your gallery. On Android 9 and older, the app may request storage permission to save images.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone’s settings.

5. Third-party components

Google ML Kit (barcode scanning). Scanning runs on your device using Google’s ML Kit library. According to Google’s published disclosure, ML Kit may collect limited technical information such as device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app information (package name and version) and performance metrics, used for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. This data is handled by Google, not by us. Learn more at developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

6. Sharing and opening links

When you tap Share, the app hands the code or file to the Android share sheet, and you decide which app receives it. When you tap Open on a scanned link, the link opens in your browser or another app on your device. Those apps and websites have their own privacy policies.

A QR code can contain any link or text. Only open links from sources you trust.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Your choices

Because we do not hold your personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to export or erase on your behalf. You are in control of everything the app stores through the settings described above. If you have any questions, contact us at the email address below.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if advertising or analytics is added), we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
