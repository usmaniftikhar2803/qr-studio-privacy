2 What we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.
  • We do not upload the content of the codes you scan or create to any server of ours.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking inside the app, and we do not sell your personal information.

3 What is stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • History: the text of codes you scanned or chose to save, their type, date and your favorites.
  • Settings: your chosen language.

You can delete your history at any time in Settings → Delete all history. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app also removes this data.

4 Permissions the app uses

CameraTo scan QR codes and barcodes. The camera image is processed on your device.
Photos / mediaOnly when you pick an image to scan or use as a logo (through the system picker), or save a QR code to your gallery. Android 9 and older may ask for storage permission to save images.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone’s settings.

5 Third-party components

Google ML Kit (barcode scanning). Scanning runs on your device using Google’s ML Kit library. According to Google’s published disclosure, ML Kit may collect limited technical information such as device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app information (package name and version) and performance metrics, used for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. This data is handled by Google, not by us.

Google AdMob (advertising). The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your location and choices, ads may be personalized. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (usually under Google → Ads). This data is collected and used by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us.

6 Sharing and opening links

When you tap Share, the app hands the code or file to the Android share sheet, and you decide which app receives it. When you tap Open on a scanned link, the link opens in your browser or another app on your device. Those apps and websites have their own privacy policies.

A QR code can contain any link or text. Only open links from sources you trust.
